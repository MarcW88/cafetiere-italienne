## Editorial thesis

The 12-cup label marks a large-capacity tier, but not one standardized volume. Current official sources range from ~595 ml brewed volume and 600 ml nominal capacity to ~717 ml water quantity, so the reader should use roughly 0.6 L as a practical landmark only after checking what the exact manufacturer figure means.

1. **Direct answer** — 12 cups is a large format; ~0.6 L is a useful landmark on some current models, not a universal conversion.

3. **Decision boundary** — when 9, 10 or 12 cups is more coherent based on target quantity.
