# Affiliate-value gate — /capacites/cafetiere-italienne-2-tasses/

Date: 2026-09-13
Status: PASS
Reason for gate: the page can influence purchase sizing even though it must not rank products.

## 1. Unique value already present

- translates a vague commercial label into a verified order of magnitude without claiming a universal conversion;
- separates brewed-volume evidence from nominal-capacity evidence;
- exposes the small-format induction detection constraint;
- routes the reader to adjacent capacity bands when the 2-cup band does not fit;
- preserves the capacity/product boundary.

## 2. Merchant-like content to remove

- large product-by-product comparison tables as the central page structure;
- feature-listing that does not change the size decision;
- merchant language such as “ideal”, “best”, “premium”, “easy to clean” or design/comfort claims unless independently decision-relevant and evidenced;
- availability/price claims, which are not needed for this page's role.

## 3. Missing decision information

The exact workflow decision artifact has now supplied the missing information:
- the user's target moka volume in ml is the first decision variable;
- induction/base detection is a hard constraint when relevant;
- ~60 ml and ~130 ml are useful adjacent Bialetti landmarks when ~85–100 ml is clearly too much or too little;
- product selection starts only after the size/hob decision.

## 4. Evidence needed

Sufficient primary evidence exists for the capacity decision:
- Bialetti Moka Express 1/2/3/4 brewed-volume landmarks;
- Bialetti Venus 2 and Moka Induction 2 volume/base/induction facts;
- Cecotec 2-cup/100 ml capacity and hob compatibility;
- Barazzoni existence of a 2-cup induction variant if needed as market corroboration.

No user-review or hands-on evidence is required because no experiential product judgment is planned.

## 5. Claims that must be qualified

- `~85–100 ml` applies to the verified references, not every 2-cup moka on the market;
- `100 ml capacity` must not be relabeled as `100 ml brewed yield`;
- induction compatibility does not guarantee detection on every hob;
- Bialetti's 1/3/4-cup ml values are adjacent landmarks for that range, not a universal cross-brand conversion;
- no people-equivalence claim.

## 6. Recommended comparison criteria

For this capacity page, product-comparison criteria are intentionally NOT used. The page should use only the criteria needed for the capacity decision:

1. published volume/capacity metric;
2. exact size label/variant;
3. hob compatibility when mandatory;
4. base/detection constraint when published;
5. adjacent capacity band.

If material, maintenance, price, ergonomics or product-specific trade-offs become primary, route to `/comparatifs/`.

## Affiliate integrity conclusion

PASS. The page remains useful with zero affiliate links and does not depend on monetizable product availability.
