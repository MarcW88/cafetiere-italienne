## Adjacent-size evidence

Bialetti Moka Express provides a clean same-family landmark:
- 9 cup: ~410 ml approximate brewed coffee volume;
- 12 cup: ~595 ml approximate brewed coffee volume.

The already reviewed 10-cup page documents current 10-cup examples around ~460–500 ml depending on model and metric.

## Decision facts that survive fact-check

1. `12 cups` is a useful large-capacity label, but not a universal ml conversion.
2. On current primary sources, Bialetti gives ~595 ml brewed volume, Cecotec gives 600 ml nominal capacity, and Forever gives ~717 ml water quantity for a 12-cup variant. Those metrics must stay separate.
3. For a reader targeting roughly 0.6 L of prepared coffee, the 12-cup tier can be relevant, but the exact model still needs verification.
4. Cooktop compatibility is model-specific: current 12-cup products include both induction-capable and non-induction examples.
5. Product material, handling, maintenance, price and exact model preference belong to `/comparatifs/` after the capacity decision.
