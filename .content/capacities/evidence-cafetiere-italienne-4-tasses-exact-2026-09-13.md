## Verified current examples

- Bialetti Moka Induction 4: ~150 ml approximate brewed coffee volume; base ~10.0 cm; induction subject to cooktop detection.
- Bialetti Venus 4: ~170 ml approximate brewed coffee volume; base ~9.5 cm; induction subject to cooktop detection.
- Bialetti Moka Express 4: ~185 ml approximate brewed coffee volume.
- Cecotec Moking 400: 200 ml nominal capacity; 4 cups; induction/gas/electric/ceramic advertised.
- Grønenberg 4 cup: 200 ml; induction field from Ø 9.5 cm.

## Adjacent-size evidence

Bialetti Moka Express provides a clean same-family reference:
- 3 cup: ~130 ml approximate brewed volume;
- 4 cup: ~185 ml;
- 6 cup: ~250 ml.

These are not universal moka conversions. They are useful only as one documented family showing how adjacent nominal sizes can differ materially.

## Decision facts that survive fact-check

1. On current verified 4-cup references, the published volume spans roughly 150 to 200 ml.
2. The spread is large enough that the label `4 tasses` alone cannot determine whether a specific model matches a 150 ml or 200 ml need.
3. Manufacturer metrics differ: Bialetti explicitly publishes approximate brewed coffee volume; Cecotec publishes capacity; other manufacturers may phrase the quantity differently.
4. Induction remains exact-model and cooktop-specific. A compatible model can still require a minimum base/field diameter.
5. Product material, dishwasher compatibility, price and repairability belong to `/comparatifs/` once the volume requirement is resolved.
