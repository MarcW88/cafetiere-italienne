## Verified current examples

- Bialetti Venus 6: ~235 ml approximate brewed coffee volume; base ~10.5 cm; induction with cooktop-detection caveat.
- Bialetti Moka Express 6: ~250 ml approximate brewed coffee volume; classic aluminium, not direct induction.
- Bialetti Moka Exclusive Induction 6: ~250 ml approximate brewed coffee volume; base ~10.5 cm; induction.
- Bialetti Moka Induction 6: ~280 ml approximate brewed coffee volume; base ~11.5 cm; induction with cooktop-detection caveat.
- CRISTEL Torino 6: 0.30 L capacity; bottom 10 cm; all hobs; CRISTEL defines the 6 cups as 5 cl espresso cups.
- Grønenberg 6: 300 ml; induction field from Ø 10.5 cm.
- Cecotec Moking 600: 300 ml nominal capacity; 6 cups; induction/gas/electric/ceramic advertised.

## Adjacent-size evidence

Bialetti Moka Express gives one same-family scale:
- 4 cup: ~185 ml approximate brewed volume;
- 6 cup: ~250 ml;
- 9 cup: ~410 ml.

These values are Bialetti-specific and must not be promoted as universal cup-to-ml conversions.

## Decision facts that survive fact-check

1. The current verified 6-cup examples span roughly 235–300 ml.
2. That spread is materially large enough that the nominal `6 tasses` label alone cannot determine whether a model fits a 240 ml or 300 ml target.
3. Metric type matters: some manufacturers publish approximate brewed coffee volume, others capacity/containment.
4. The 6-cup tier has many induction-capable examples, but cooktop compatibility remains exact-model and sometimes diameter-dependent.
5. A reader regularly below ~200 ml should reconsider the 4-cup tier; a reader substantially above 300 ml should investigate 9/10 cups rather than forcing the 6-cup label.
6. Material, maintenance, price and design belong to `/comparatifs/` once capacity is resolved.
