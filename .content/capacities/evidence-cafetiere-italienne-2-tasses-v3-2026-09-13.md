# Evidence brief — Cafetière italienne 2 tasses V3

Date: 2026-09-13
Route: `/capacites/cafetiere-italienne-2-tasses/`
Scope: capacity decision only; no product ranking.

## Verified primary evidence

### Bialetti Moka Express
Source: https://www.bialetti.co.nz/products/moka-express
- 1 cup: ~60 ml approximate brewed coffee volume; base ~7 cm.
- 2 cup: ~90 ml approximate brewed coffee volume; base ~8 cm.
- 3 cup: ~130 ml approximate brewed coffee volume; base ~9 cm.
- 4 cup: ~185 ml approximate brewed coffee volume; base ~9.5 cm.
- Classic Moka Express is not directly induction compatible.
- Bialetti states that moka pots should be brewed full and that size should match the usual serve.

### Bialetti Venus 2
Source: https://www.bialetti.co.nz/products/bialetti-venus-induction-copper
- 2 cup: ~85 ml approximate brewed coffee volume.
- base ~8 cm.
- explicit exception: 2 cup Venus is NOT suitable for induction; 4 and 6 are.

### Bialetti Moka Induction 2
Source: https://www.bialetti.co.nz/products/bialetti-moka-induction-bi-layer-black
- 2 cup: ~100 ml approximate brewed coffee volume.
- base ~9.5 cm.
- induction compatible, but Bialetti explicitly asks users to check the cooktop manufacturer's minimum accepted base diameter.

### Cecotec Piccolina 200
Source: https://www.storececotec.fr/fr/cafetieres-italiennes/piccolina-200
- 2 cups.
- 100 ml capacity.
- stainless steel.
- manufacturer states compatibility with induction, gas, electric and ceramic hobs.

### Barazzoni La Caffettiera inox 2
Source: https://www.barazzoni.it/en/products/la-caffettiera-caffettiera-acciaio-inox-830008002
- 2 cups.
- diameter 8.5 cm.
- induction compatible.
- Barazzoni's inox moka instructions warn that some induction cooktops may not work with the reduced diameter of the coffee pot and instruct users to read the cooktop instructions.
Instructions: https://www.barazzoni.it/media/2023/07/barazzoni_istruzioni_caff_inox_2023_4474.pdf

## Decision facts

1. The verified 2-cup references occupy roughly an 85–100 ml zone.
2. This is not a universal conversion: manufacturers publish either brewed volume or nominal capacity.
3. The adjacent Bialetti Moka Express sizes give useful capacity landmarks: ~60 ml (1), ~90 ml (2), ~130 ml (3), ~185 ml (4). These are Bialetti-specific landmarks, not a universal moka standard.
4. For induction, compatibility is model-specific AND detection can remain cooktop-specific at these small base diameters.
5. Therefore the page's decision should be: target volume first; induction/detection second; product selection only after both constraints are satisfied.

## Exclusions / non-claims

- Do not say 2 cups = 2 people.
- Do not say all 2-cup moka pots are 90 or 100 ml.
- Do not rank Venus, Moka Induction, Cecotec or Barazzoni.
- Do not infer actual brewed yield from a nominal capacity claim.
- Do not claim any induction-compatible 2-cup pot will be detected by every hob.
