## Reducer / flexible-capacity evidence

The 9090 user manual reproduced by two manual libraries documents the reducer behavior explicitly:

- https://manualzz.com/doc/5066106/alessi-9090-coffee-maker-user-manual
- https://www.manualslib.fr/manual/488734/Alessi-9090.html?page=7

Documented use:

- 3-cup 9090 can use its reducer to prepare 1 cup ;
- 6-cup 9090 can use its reducer to prepare 3 cups ;
- 10-cup 9090 can use its reducer to prepare 6 cups ;
- 1-cup model has no smaller reducer mode documented.

Current Alessi product pages independently confirm that these dedicated reducer parts still exist for each relevant size:

- 3-cup reducer 17605/R: https://alessi.com/products/17605-r
- 6-cup reducer 17602/R: https://alessi.com/products/17602-r
- 10-cup reducer 17611/R: https://alessi.com/products/17611-r

This is decision-relevant because the nominal size is not always the only usable yield. It does **not** make reducers universal: each part remains tied to a specific 9090 size/reference.

## Contradiction known

Official Alessi pages/locales do not all use the same launch year: 1979 and 1980 both appear. Historical wording must therefore stay qualified as “autour de 1980” / “fin des années 1970–1980”, not presented as a falsely exact invariant date.

A second source-quality distinction is explicit: reducer **existence and compatibility** are current primary-source facts from Alessi; reducer **yield instructions** come from the 9090 manual reproduced by independent manual libraries. The draft may use that behavior, but it should preserve that evidence hierarchy rather than pretending a current product page states the full procedure.

## Context evidence

Alessi documents Compasso d'Oro and MoMA context. These facts explain design history but are not evidence of coffee performance and are not required for the product verdict.

## Experience layer

No independent hands-on claim is required. The premium decision can be evaluated from documented mechanics, manufacturing, size, induction, reducer flexibility and repairability without pretending to have tested the coffee.

## Decision impact

The page must answer whether the premium buys functional/mechanical differences the reader actually values, whether the chosen size fits the required volume/hob, whether a reducer lets one size cover a second documented yield, and whether dedicated parts remain identifiable.
