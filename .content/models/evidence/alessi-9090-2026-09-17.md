## Contradiction known

Official Alessi pages/locales do not all use the same launch year: 1979 and 1980 both appear. Historical wording must therefore stay qualified as “autour de 1980” / “fin des années 1970–1980”, not presented as a falsely exact invariant date.

## Context evidence

Alessi documents Compasso d'Oro and MoMA context. These facts explain design history but are not evidence of coffee performance and are not required for the product verdict.

## Experience layer

No independent hands-on claim is required. The premium decision can be evaluated from documented mechanics, manufacturing, size, induction and repairability without pretending to have tested the coffee.

## Decision impact

The page must answer whether the premium buys functional/mechanical differences the reader actually values, whether the chosen size fits the required volume/hob, and whether dedicated parts remain identifiable.
