## Variant evidence

The D&G family itself does not have one invariant capacity matrix:

- Sicilia-style reference: 2 cup ≈ 90 ml and 4 cup ≈ 190 ml.
- Blu Mediterraneo reference: 4 cup ≈ 150 ml and 6 cup ≈ 225 ml.

These figures prove that neither “Moka Induction 4 cups” nor “D&G Moka Induction” can be assigned one universal brewed-volume figure without naming the exact reference.

## Contradictions resolved

### Standard 2 cup

Bialetti NZ says ≈100 ml; the official Bialetti Russia shop and a current Belgian retailer say ≈90 ml for European-market references. The page therefore must not publish 100 ml as a universal current spec. Editorial resolution: `≈ 90–100 ml selon référence / marché`.

### D&G editions

The 4-cup figures vary between ≈150 and ≈190 ml across checked editions, and a checked Blu Mediterraneo 6-cup reference is ≈225 ml instead of the ≈280 ml standard reference. Keep every number tied to its edition.

### Current vs pre-2020 parts

Current Bi-Layer and pre-2020 funnels remain separate generations and must never be merged into one compatibility statement.

## Experience layer

No independent hands-on verdict is needed. The main user risk is documentary: buying from the family name without checking market/reference, hob diameter or generation.

## Decision impact

The page must prevent four errors:
1. assuming “induction compatible” means every hob will detect every size ;
2. treating a market-specific 2-cup volume as universal ;
3. transferring one edition's volume figures to another ;
4. ordering current parts for a pre-2020 model or vice versa.
