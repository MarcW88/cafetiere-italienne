## Variant evidence

Current Dolce & Gabbana Moka Induction references document different prepared-volume figures from the standard Bi-Layer black version:

- https://www.bialetti.co.nz/products/bialetti-dolce-gabbana-moka-induction-2-cup — about 90 ml for 2 cups.
- https://www.bialetti.co.nz/products/bialetti-dolce-gabbana-moka-induction-2-cup-blumed — about 190 ml for 4 cups on the consulted reference.

These figures prove that “Moka Induction 4 cups” cannot be treated as one invariant specification across every current edition.

## Contradictions / unknowns

No unresolved contradiction is allowed between current Bi-Layer and pre-2020 parts: they are explicitly separate generations. Variant volumes remain tied to the exact consulted reference.

## Experience layer

No independent hands-on verdict is needed for the current page role. The main user risk is documentary: buying from the family name without checking variant, hob diameter or generation.

## Decision impact

The page must prevent three errors:
1. assuming “induction compatible” means every hob will detect every size ;
2. transferring one edition's volume figures to another ;
3. ordering current parts for a pre-2020 model or vice versa.
