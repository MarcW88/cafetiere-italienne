## Retest finding

The v2 retest found a decision-relevant qualification gap in the 2-cup volume. The page currently presents `≈ 100 ml` as the single current figure, sourced from Bialetti New Zealand. However:

- Bialetti NZ documents the current black Bi-Layer 2 cup at about 100 ml;
- the official Bialetti Russia shop documents the Moka Induction 2020 2 cup at about 90 ml;
- a current Belgian retailer lists the European red reference at about 90 ml.

This does not justify choosing one universal number. For a Belgian page, the defensible buying guidance is `≈ 90–100 ml selon référence / marché`, while 4 cup ≈ 150 ml and 6 cup ≈ 280 ml remain consistent across the checked standard references.

The D&G layer also confirms that decorative editions are not one invariant spec: the consulted Sicilia-style 4 cup is ≈190 ml, while Blu Mediterraneo 4 cup is ≈150 ml and 6 cup ≈225 ml.

## Why this blocks PASS

The page's editorial thesis is that the exact reference matters. Publishing one unqualified 2-cup figure contradicts that thesis and can mislead a reader choosing by volume.

## Required correction

- qualify standard 2-cup volume as `≈ 90–100 ml selon référence / marché`;
- keep the 9.5 cm base figure tied to the NZ Bi-Layer reference rather than implying every market reference is identical;
- make the D&G examples explicitly edition-specific, including that some 4-cup editions remain around 150 ml while others are around 190 ml;
- rerun post-draft and PUBLISH_REVIEW after the rendered page is corrected.

## Publication
