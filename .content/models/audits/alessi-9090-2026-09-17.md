# PAGE_AUDIT — Alessi 9090

Date : 2026-09-17

## Role / search intent

PRODUCT page for a premium/design moka. The reader needs to know what concrete mechanics and manufacturing choices they buy, which size fits, whether the small model will work on induction, and whether one larger size can legitimately cover a second smaller-volume use case.

## SEO / cluster

- Primary entity: Alessi 9090.
- Distinct from Alessi brand hub: this page resolves a model-level purchase decision.
- Distinct from generic design/inox comparisons: lever closure, 1-cup induction threshold, reducer behavior and dedicated parts are model-specific.

## Value already present

The page documents lever closure, widened base, anti-drip spout, 1/3/6/10 capacities, the 90 mm induction warning, dedicated parts, the 1979/1980 source contradiction and the 2026 CP edition without presenting prestige as coffee performance.

## Decision gap found during retest

The page mentions a reducer only as a spare part. It does not consume the documented **capacity flexibility** of the 9090 system:

- 3-cup model → reducer for 1 cup ;
- 6-cup model → reducer for 3 cups ;
- 10-cup model → reducer for 6 cups.

This can change the purchase decision. A reader alternating between 3 and 6 cups may rationally prefer a 6-cup 9090 with its dedicated reducer instead of assuming two separate sizes are required. The same applies to 3→1 and 10→6.

The omission is therefore decision-relevant, not a completeness nicety.

## Action

Treat the current PASS as stale for this model. Add reducer behavior to evidence, ledger, JTBD/decision criteria, content brief and rendered copy. Keep the exact reducer reference tied to the exact 9090 size; do not imply universality.

## Publication

Keep `noindex,follow` until the corrected draft passes post-draft fact-check, research-to-draft coverage and both machine validators.
